QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is vSuite ProcessFlow?

It is a configurable approach to connecting document intake, extraction, validation, review, and output. The capabilities available in a deployment depend on configuration and implementation.

Which document types can be processed?

Invoices and Bills of Lading are shown as fictional examples. Supported document types, fields, and rules would be agreed during configuration.

Are the dashboard and integrations live?

No. The dashboard is an interactive local illustration using fictional data. Enterprise connections require separate configuration and implementation.

How do approvals and exceptions fit into the process?

A configured workflow can route failed checks and decisions requiring authorization to the appropriate people for review before further processing.

What role can AI play?

AI assistance can be designed for document understanding and suggested processing steps, with appropriate rules, permissions, and human oversight. No live AI service is running on this page.
