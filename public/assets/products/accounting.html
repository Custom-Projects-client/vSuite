FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, Answered Carefully

This page describes intended capability areas and conceptual interfaces. A demonstration should confirm what is implemented for your use case.

What is vSuite Accounting?

It is presented as a connected accounting and financial-management solution from Vishva Digital Technologies. Exact delivered scope should be confirmed.

Does vSuite Accounting support a general ledger and chart of accounts?

General Ledger and Chart of Accounts are core capability areas described here. Ask for a demonstration of the available implementation and configuration.

Can vSuite Accounting manage accounts payable and receivable?

Payables and receivables are included in the intended accounting workflow. Specific invoice, balance, and approval processes depend on implementation.

Does it support bank reconciliation?

Bank Reconciliation is a described capability area. Bank-data availability, matching, and review processes depend on configured integrations and controls.

Can it generate financial statements?

Trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow reporting are described here. Confirm available reports, data sources, and review requirements.

Does it support multiple companies and currencies?

Multi-company, multi-entity, and multi-currency accounting are intended areas. Currency treatment and group-reporting rules require explicit confirmation.

Can accounting activities be automated?

Configured journals, allocations, schedules, and close tasks may support automation. Authorization and review should remain appropriate to the activity.

How can AI help with accounting?

AI could suggest coding, matching, summaries, explanations, and review priorities. Its output is not a verified financial fact and requires validation.

What is the Financial Close Agent?

It is a proposed controlled workflow concept for reviewing close status, recommending next steps, and carrying out only authorized, implemented actions.

Does it support GST, VAT, and e-invoicing?

These are described capability areas. Jurisdictional configuration, external integration, and compliance validation must be confirmed; none is guaranteed here.

Can it integrate with vSuite Billing?

Billing-to-Accounting Integration is a conceptual connection shown on this page. Confirm the available connector, mapping, and implementation scope.

How can I request a demonstration?

Request a demo and our team at Vishva Digital Technologies will get back to you to discuss your requirements.
