AI & Agentic AI

Assistive intelligence for hospital teams

Generative AI and task-oriented agents help staff find information and complete routine work faster.

AI Assistant

Ask questions about schedules, bed availability, stock or reports in plain language and get quick answers.

Agentic Workflows

Agents prepare appointment reminders, draft discharge summaries and flag incomplete bills for staff to review and approve.

Operational Insights

Dashboards on admissions, occupancy, waiting times and revenue help management plan ahead.

AI output is assistive: clinical and financial decisions remain with qualified hospital staff.
