AI Commerce Agent

A configured commerce agent can assist with permitted tasks, operating under configured policies, permissions, and approval controls—not unrestricted autonomous transactions.

Permitted assistance may include

  • Reviewing commerce activity
  • Summarizing orders requiring attention
  • Identifying product information gaps
  • Suggesting customer communication
  • Highlighting unusual order patterns

Controlled by design

What an agent can see, recommend, or do depends on the available data and its configured scope. Actions requiring approval must wait for the appropriate authorization; outcomes should be recorded for review.

Responsible AI, Configured for Control

CONTROLS & HUMAN OVERSIGHT
  • AI outputs depend on available data, configured logic, and applicable permissions.
  • Fraud alerts require appropriate review.
  • Payments, refunds, and order changes remain subject to configured authorization and approval controls.

These examples do not guarantee sales growth, accurate predictions in all cases, or unrestricted autonomous transactions.
