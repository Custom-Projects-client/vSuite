FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, Answered Carefully

These answers describe capability areas. Confirm delivered functionality and configuration for your organization.

What is vSuite Human Resources?

It is presented as a connected HR platform from Vishva Digital Technologies. Confirm the implemented scope in a demonstration.

Which HR functions does it cover?

The described areas include people records, recruitment, onboarding, attendance, leave, performance, compensation, self-service, analytics, and AI assistance.

Can it manage employee profiles and employment information?

Profiles and employment information are described capability areas, subject to appropriate permissions and configuration.

Does it support recruitment and applicant tracking?

Recruitment workflows and applicant tracking are described here. Confirm the available stages and process setup.

Can it organize employee onboarding?

Configurable checklists, document review, and orientation steps can be organized; human verification remains important.

Does it support attendance, leave, shifts, and timesheets?

These are described HR areas. Policies, approvals, and available records depend on configuration.

Can managers conduct performance reviews and track goals?

Goals and review workflows are described. Reviews should use context and human judgment, not a single score.

Does it support salary and compensation management?

Controlled compensation planning and review are described; no payroll calculation or payment is claimed.

What can employees do through the self-service portal?

Depending on permissions, employees could view information, request leave, access documents, and follow HR requests.

How can AI assist HR teams?

AI could draft, summarize, or surface relevant information for authorized users, subject to validation and human review.

What is the HR Operations Agent?

It is a controlled workflow concept for suggesting next steps and carrying out only authorized, implemented actions with required approvals.

Can AI make recruitment or employment decisions automatically?

No. AI must not independently hire, reject, promote, or terminate; people remain responsible for decisions.

Does it support workforce analytics?

Workforce, recruitment, and performance analytics are described areas. Available views depend on permitted data and implementation.

Can it integrate with other vSuite products?

The ecosystem diagram shows conceptual connections where supported, not confirmed synchronization or connectors.
